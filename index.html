Privacy Policy

Automated School Bell · Last updated: June 15, 2026

Automated School Bell is designed to help schools and users configure bell schedules and ringing behavior without collecting or sharing personal data.

App purpose

Automated School Bell provides configurable school bell schedules and ringing behavior. Users can set schedules, timing, and related bell preferences so the app can ring according to the configured school timetable.

Data collection

Automated School Bell does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. Schedule and ringing configuration are used by the app to provide its core bell scheduling functionality.

Activation

The app may make activation requests to https://activate.white-board.io/activation. These requests are used only for activation-related functionality.

Third-party sharing

Automated School Bell does not share personal data with third parties. Apart from activation requests described above, the app does not send data to other services or people.

Children's privacy

The app is intended to support school bell scheduling. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Contact and support

No dedicated support contact is currently listed for Automated School Bell. If contact information becomes available, this privacy policy will be updated to include it.

Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
